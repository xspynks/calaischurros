# Campaigns are configured Templates

**Status:** accepted

A Member picks a Template and fills its fields: brand, copy, Prizes, lead form. The product publishes one Experience for that Campaign. It does not offer a freeform canvas where someone places arbitrary blocks, the way a general presentation tool does.

Genially was the reference for "a non-developer can create and share a thing." The slice we take from that idea is a Catalog of small interactions, not a general editor.

## Consequences

A new interaction is a new Template: a runtime module, a set of Studio fields, and a Catalog entry. Prize, Play, Coupon, Lead, and the Share URL stay as they are. The MVP Catalog has two playable Templates, Raspadinha and Roleta, plus named entries that cannot be started yet. Dozens of Templates are how the Catalog grows after this plan, one Template at a time, on the same ticket shape as Roleta.
