# The Organization owns the promotion rules

**Status:** accepted

An Experience is a promotional Reveal. The Organization chooses the Prizes, the weights, the validity line, the redemption instructions, and the consent sentence. The product stores those words and shows them to the Visitor. It does not decide whether a Campaign is a lawful promotion, sweepstakes, or lottery in the Organization's jurisdiction, and it does not add age gates or gambling controls.

## Consequences

Copy on the Experience comes from the Campaign. The product must not invent a promise the Member did not write. Coupon redemption at a physical store, as in the Calais footer, is an instruction on the page plus a redeemed mark in the Studio. Payment for the Prize happens outside the product.
